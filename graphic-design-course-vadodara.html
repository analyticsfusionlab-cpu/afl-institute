Who can join the Graphic Design Course in Vadodara?

Students, graduates, beginners, social media creators, marketing professionals and aspiring freelancers can join.

No. Graphic design focuses on visual communication, layout, typography, color and software skills.

Yes. Photoshop is covered for image editing, compositing, social media creatives and digital design.

Yes. Illustrator is used for vector graphics, logo design, icons and brand identity work.
