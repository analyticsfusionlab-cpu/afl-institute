Frequently Asked Questions

Is this service for working professionals?

Yes. This page is intended for people already working in IT or technology-related roles who want focused technical mentoring and guidance.

Can I get support for Databricks and PySpark?

You can enquire about Databricks, Apache Spark, PySpark, Delta Lake, SQL, data pipelines and related data engineering topics. The exact scope is confirmed after discussing your requirement and mentor availability.

Do you support professionals outside India?

Remote enquiries are welcome worldwide. Session timing and availability are discussed according to time zones.

No. AFL provides explanation, mentoring and troubleshooting guidance so you can understand and carry out your own responsibilities. We do not impersonate staff or perform unauthorized work.

No. Never share passwords, access tokens, confidential company data or proprietary source code. Use sanitized examples and only employer-approved tools and environments.

Requirements are reviewed first. Topics, mentor availability, session format and timing are confirmed before any engagement.
