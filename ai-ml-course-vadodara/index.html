AI & Machine Learning Course in Vadodara – FAQs

Common questions about Python, ML, Deep Learning, NLP and Generative AI.

Can a beginner join the AI & Machine Learning Course?

Yes. The course starts with Python, data handling and statistics before moving into machine learning and advanced AI topics.

What is the difference between AI and Machine Learning?

Artificial Intelligence is the broader field of building systems that perform intelligent tasks. Machine Learning is a major AI approach where systems learn patterns from data.

Is Python required for AI/ML?

Python is one of the main languages used for practical AI/ML workflows, so it is included from the foundation stage.

Does the course include Deep Learning?

Yes. Neural-network fundamentals, CNN concepts and other deep-learning foundations are included.

Does the course include NLP?

Yes. Text preprocessing, TF-IDF concepts, sentiment analysis and embedding concepts are included.

Does the course include Generative AI and LLMs?

Yes. Generative AI fundamentals, LLM concepts, prompt engineering, embeddings and RAG concepts are included.

What Machine Learning algorithms are covered?

The roadmap includes regression, classification, decision trees, random forest, KNN, SVM concepts and clustering.

Does the course include model evaluation?

Yes. Accuracy, precision, recall, F1 score, confusion matrix, regression metrics, cross-validation and tuning concepts are included.

What projects will I build?

Projects include churn prediction, house-price prediction, customer segmentation, sentiment analysis, loan-risk prediction, image classification concepts, RAG/document Q&A concepts and a capstone.

Do I need advanced mathematics?

No advanced mastery is required before starting, but statistics, probability and mathematical reasoning become increasingly important as you progress.

What career roles can I explore?

Depending on prior education, experience and demonstrated skills, learners may explore AI/ML Intern, Junior ML Engineer, AI Associate, Python/ML Analyst and related roles.
