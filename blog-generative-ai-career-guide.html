Generative AI Career Guide for Beginners

A practical introduction to Generative AI, LLM concepts and the skills beginners should learn before building AI applications.

What is Generative AI?

Generative AI refers to AI systems that can generate text, images, code and other content based on learned patterns and user prompts.

What are Large Language Models?

Large Language Models are AI models trained on large amounts of text data and designed to understand and generate human-like language.

What skills should beginners learn?

A strong foundation in Python, data handling, basic Machine Learning, prompting, embeddings and application concepts is useful before moving into advanced Gen AI projects.

What is RAG?

Retrieval-Augmented Generation connects an AI model with external information sources so that responses can use relevant retrieved context.

Career direction

Beginners can prepare for AI application support, data and AI associate roles, prompt-oriented workflows and junior AI/ML opportunities depending on their technical background and portfolio.
