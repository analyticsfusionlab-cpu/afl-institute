Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need previous teaching experience?

Teaching experience can be helpful, but strong practical industry knowledge and the ability to explain concepts clearly are also important.

Can working professionals apply as part-time trainers?

Yes. AFL Institute welcomes applications from working professionals whose availability and expertise match current training requirements.

Can I teach only on weekends?

Weekend opportunities may be available depending on current batches and trainer requirements.

Are online trainer opportunities available?

Online or hybrid opportunities may be considered based on the subject, requirement and current course delivery model.

What happens after I submit my application?

AFL Institute can review your profile and contact you if your experience and availability match a current or upcoming requirement.

Can I collaborate only as an industry mentor?

Yes. Professionals may also contribute through guest lectures, mock interviews, workshops, project mentoring or other industry-interaction activities.
