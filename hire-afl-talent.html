Employer FAQs

What type of candidates can we hire from AFL Institute?

AFL Institute trains candidates across Data Analytics, Data Science, AI/ML, Python, SQL, Power BI, AWS Cloud, DevOps, Digital Marketing, SEO, Graphic Design and development-oriented skills.

Can we share a specific skill requirement?

Yes. You can share the role, required tools or technologies, job location, experience level, number of openings and preferred joining timeline.

Are candidates freshers or experienced professionals?

Candidate profiles may include freshers, career switchers and working professionals. Availability depends on current batches and learner profiles.

Can AFL Institute arrange interviews?

AFL Institute can help coordinate candidate sharing and interview communication. Final assessment and selection remain with the hiring organization.

Can companies hire interns as well?

Yes. Employers can share internship requirements as well as full-time or entry-level job opportunities.

How do we get candidate profiles?

Submit the employer form or contact AFL Institute on WhatsApp with your requirement. Suitable available profiles can then be discussed with you.
