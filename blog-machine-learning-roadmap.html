Machine Learning Roadmap for Beginners

Learn the right order to build your machine learning foundation.

Start with Strong Fundamentals

Begin with Excel, data cleaning, formulas and business reporting. Then move to SQL for database queries, Power BI for dashboards and Python for deeper analysis.

Build Practical Projects

Projects are important because they demonstrate how you apply tools to real problems. Retail, HR, sales and customer analytics projects are useful portfolio examples.

Create a Resume and Portfolio

Show your tools, projects, dashboards and business problem-solving skills clearly. Keep your resume focused on measurable analytics work and practical outcomes.

Prepare for Interviews

Practice Excel formulas, SQL queries, Power BI concepts, data interpretation and scenario-based questions. Be ready to explain your projects from problem to result.
